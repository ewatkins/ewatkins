# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 17, 2026_

This policy covers the sign-in used for the self-hosted services I run under `ewatkins.dev`, such as Keycloak (`keycloak.ewatkins.dev`) and the applications protected by Cloudflare Access. These are personal services, not a commercial product, and access is limited to people I have explicitly authorized.

## Information collected

When you sign in with Google, these services receive only the following basic profile information from your Google account:

- Your name
- Your email address
- Your profile picture
- A unique Google account identifier

No other Google account data is requested. The services do not read your email, contacts, files, calendar or any other Google data.

## How it is used

This information is used only to:

- Verify your identity when you sign in
- Decide whether your account is allowed to access a service
- Keep you signed in for the length of your session

## Sharing

Your information is never sold, rented or used for advertising. It is not shared with anyone, except with the services that make sign-in work:

- **Google**, which handles the sign-in itself
- **Cloudflare**, which runs Cloudflare Access for some of these services

## Storage and retention

Your account details are stored in my self-hosted Keycloak instance and in Cloudflare Access session logs, and are kept only as long as you have access. Sign-in sessions expire automatically.

## Your choices

- You can revoke access at any time in your Google account under [Third-party apps & services](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).
- You can ask me to delete your account and associated data by emailing me.

Questions or requests: [eric@ewatkins.dev](mailto:eric@ewatkins.dev)

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
